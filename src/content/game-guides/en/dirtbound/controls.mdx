## Desktop keyboard controls

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>W</kbd> or <kbd>↑</kbd> | Accelerate |
| <kbd>S</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> | Brake or reverse |
| <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> | Steer |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Handbrake |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | Change camera |
| <kbd>R</kbd> | Reset the car; adds a five-second penalty |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Pause |

<Callout type="warn" title="Reset deliberately">
  Resetting is useful when you are stuck, but it adds five seconds to your time. See the modes and penalties guide for what can also trigger a reset.
</Callout>

## Mobile controls

On mobile, touch and drag left or right to steer. DirtBound accelerates the car automatically, so there is no separate accelerator button to find. Use short, gentle drags before a corner rather than waiting until the car is already wide.

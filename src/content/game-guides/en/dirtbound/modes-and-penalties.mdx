## Why did I get a time penalty?

| What happened | Time added | What to do next |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| You went off the road, got stuck, or pressed <kbd>R</kbd> | +5 seconds | Recover early and use reset only when it saves more than five seconds. |
| You hit a spectator | +10 seconds | Leave more margin through crowd zones; the penalty can erase a clean section. |

If you are only trying to finish a stage, clean driving usually matters more than pushing every corner. Grass also slows the car, so a short-looking off-road line is not always faster.

## Multiplayer, leaderboards, and accounts

You can drive solo stages without an account. The official game uses Google sign-in for online features, including multiplayer lobbies, leaderboards, and publishing circuits. For a shared challenge without a lobby, send a friend the generated stage URL so you can attempt the same road.

## Performance on a slower device

If the game stutters, try the **Low** graphics preset in Settings first. It reduces visual details such as scenery, shadows, and smoke.
